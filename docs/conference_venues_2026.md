# Recommended Conference Venues for 2026 Publication

## Research Alignment
**Focus Areas:** Ontological Induction, Sequence Modeling, Sheaf-Theoretic Reward Spaces, Neuro-Symbolic AI, Applied Category Theory.

This research sits at the intersection of **Category Theory**, **Language Modeling**, and **Safe RL**. The venues below are selected to maximize receptivity to these specific methodologies.
